# Chest Rush · Story & Lore Asset Library (Inspired by "Mysterious Awakening")

> Purpose: To provide a fleshed-out world skeleton for the "Mobile Tower + 3 Specter Loadout + Looting Items & Extracting via Doors" gameplay loop.  
> Principle: **Borrow the vibe and mechanical skeleton, but do not copy the original proper nouns for the commercial release**; the names below can serve as internal codenames, and official in-game names can be coined before launch.  
> Gameplay Anchor (Based on `2.next_phase.md`): The protagonist is a mobile tower; 3 "Friendly Specters" auto-battle at the start of each run; "Enemy Specters" spawn in waves; after looting key items, you extract through the "Door".

---

## 0. One-Sentence World Pitch

In an alternate modern city undergoing a supernatural awakening, ordinary people cannot see the rules. **Specter Hosts** use their own bodies as cages, harnessing evil spirits in exchange for power. They dive through "Doors" into **Specter Domain Fragments** (Levels) to scavenge key relics that can suppress the awakening—the price being: every time they use this power, the specter inside them wakes up a little more.

## I. Directly Implementable World Skeleton

### 1.1 Core Concepts (Recommend turning all into system glossary entries)

| Concept | Novel Vibe | Game Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| **Awakening** | The more a specter is used, the more it wakes up, eventually backfiring on the host. | In-run "Awakening Gauge": Excessive upgrading/prolonged stay increases enemy wave intensity, or pushes friendly specters into a "Loss of Control Warning" state. |
| **Harnessing** | Humans trapping specters in their bodies as weapons. | = Equipment slots; 3 Specter slots; change loadout between levels. |
| **Specter Domain** | An alternate space/polluted zone expanded by a specter. | = The Level itself; fog, decay textures, and compressed vision are all domain manifestations. |
| **Thralls** | The dead assimilated by the domain, mimicking their living selves but mindless. | = Low-tier mob templates; humanoid but with stiff behaviors; drop "Ghost Money" (in-run upgrade currency) on kill. |
| **Door** | Passageways leading to alternate spaces/events. | = Extraction Point; can also be designed as "Fake Door" traps summoned by the Door-Knocking Specter. |
| **Triggers + Kill Rules** | Every specter has fixed triggers and execution methods. | = Enemy mechanic manual; players rely on observation to counter them, rather than mindless DPS. |
| **Threat Levels C/B/A/S** | Categorized by hazard radius, not strictly single-target strength. | = Level Threat Tags + Boss Tags; C = Stationary, B = Roaming, A = Area Control, S = Level-end Disaster. |

**Threat Level Implementation Suggestions (Tied to Wave Curves)**

- **C - Restricted Class**: Fixed spawn points / low movement speed mobs, suitable for tutorial levels.  
- **B - Dangerous Class**: Roaming, capable of spreading (splitting/infectious marks), mid-game pressure source.  
- **A - Disaster Class**: Comes with "Area Rules" (rooting, head-swapping, damage transfer), Elites / Mini-Bosses.  
- **S - Cataclysmic Class**: Level-end or time-limited event Bosses that alter the rules of the entire map (time rewinding, map-wide starvation, map-wide dream states, etc.).

### 1.2 Story Background (Tone-setting, no need for tedious plots)

- The world appears normal on the surface, but in the shadows, an "Interpol-style" Specter Host organization is archiving, containing, and sending people to their deaths.  
- The protagonist is not a saintly savior; they are a **pragmatist who wants to survive, get stronger, and conveniently pull the world back from the brink of collapse a little bit**.  
- Each level "Specter Domain" = A fragment left behind by a supernatural event: schools, malls, deserted villages, buses, hotels... The level themes are directly derived from these events.  
- Key Items = Relics recorded in the organization's archives that can "pin down a specter / deceive a specter / trade for a life" (vibes like Coffin Nails, Ghost Candles, Scapegoat Dolls, scraps of Human Skin Paper).

**Gameplay Integration**: A 10-second subtitle/radio voiceover at the start of the run is enough; reserve long narratives for the Bestiary and post-clear archives to avoid interrupting the 5–10 minute match pacing.

## II. The Five Specters You Proposed: Original Counterparts + Balance & Gameplay Suggestions

### 2.1 Door-Knocking Specter (Enemy · A-Disaster Class · High HP)

**Original Skeleton**: An old man in black who doesn't push doors open, only knocks in a fixed rhythm; the knocking triggers a curse-like death; can expand a Specter Domain; generates massive amounts of Thralls.

**Your Design (Keep)**  
Roams the map → A "Door" spawns out of thin air in front of it → Knocks → Units in the current area with a lower level than it (including the protagonist and low-tier enemy specters) are rooted for 5s → AoE damage to all when the root ends.

**Refinement Suggestions**

| Point | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| Oppression | 1.5s before knocking, there is a visual "Doorframe materializing" + "Knock-knock" audio cue, providing a window for positioning. |
| Level Gap | "Rooted only if lower level" is excellent; players can use in-run upgrades to temporarily boost their "Anti-Knock Level". |
| Friendly Fire | The root also affects low-tier enemy specters → can be a risk-reward mechanic: "Use the Knocker to clear the board, but take a hit yourself." |
| Counters | The Phantom Noose hoists it up to interrupt the knocking cast; Scapegoat redirects the damage to mobs; Ravenous Specter devours its summoned Thralls instantly when the root ends. |
| Boss Variant | The 3rd knock triggers a "Temporary Domain Expansion": The area's fog thickens, vision -1, lasts 8s. |

**Level Role**: Mid-to-late game Elite / Area Controller; its appearance forces players to abandon their current looting spot.

---

### 2.2 Phantom Noose / Ghost Rope (Friendly Starter Weapon · Starts Low-tier, Evolvable)

**Original Skeleton**: An invisible specter infesting a straw rope, strangling necks without warning; once tied, even fierce specters struggle to break free; the host is also choked in return (a double-edged sword).

**Your Design (Keep)**  
Hoists up several surrounding enemy specters, applying Root + DoT; DPS ∝ Noose upgrade level vs Target level.

**Refinement Suggestions**

| Point | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| Feel | Auto-targeting prioritizes "Lowest HP / Elites currently casting". |
| Cap | Max concurrent hoists = 1 + Upgrade Level; against A-Class or higher, it can only root, not instakill, emphasizing crowd control (CC). |
| Double-Edged Flavor | At max upgrade stacks, randomly triggers "Backlash": Protagonist's movement speed drops briefly in exchange for higher DPS. |
| Synergies | Hoist → Head-Swapping Specter steals abilities; Hoist → Ravenous Specter feeds safely. |

**Positioning**: Close-to-mid-range CC tool, teaching players to "Control first, then DPS".

---

### 2.3 Scapegoat Specter (Enemy · A-Class · High HP/ATK + Damage Transfer)

**Original Vibe**: Substitute/Scapegoat, replaces you after strangulation, mirrors require a substitute to manifest, etc.—"Transferring death to others."

**Your Design (Keep)**  
Passively transfers received damage to surrounding enemy specters; set a transfer cap for balance.

**Refinement Suggestions**

| Point | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| Readability | When taking damage, "black lines snap to nearby mobs"; mobs display a "Scapegoat" mark over their heads. |
| Cap | Max damage transferred per second has a cap; or enters a 2s stagger (vulnerability window) after max transfer charges are depleted. |
| Counters | Clear surrounding mobs before focusing the main body; or use the Phantom Noose to hoist mobs out of its radius. |
| Variant | "Perfect Scapegoat": On death, possesses the nearest mob to revive at full HP once (strictly once). |

**Level Role**: An elite that teaches players to "Kill the adds before hitting the boss."

---

### 2.4 Ravenous Specter (Friendly Starter Weapon · High-tier Pet · Scaling)

**Original Skeleton**: Ghost infant parasitism → bursts from the body → eats humans to grow; can eat specters; the source of S-Class events; even involves time resets.

**Your Design (Keep)**  
A "cute" Ravenous Specter accompanies the protagonist, actively rushing to devour enemy specters; grows stronger the more it eats.

**Refinement Suggestions**

| Point | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| Gap Moe | Visually cute, but reveals a grotesque, terrifying maw when eating (Horror Comedy). |
| Scaling | Eats C-Class → Minor permanent (in-run) damage buff; Eats B-Class → Attack Speed; Eats A-Class → Unlocks "Elite Munching" but feeding time increases. |
| Risk | Rooted while feeding; if interrupted by the Door-Knocking Specter's root, it spits the target out and loses a stack of growth. |
| S-Class Easter Egg (Late Game) | When maxed out, grants a short "Mini-Reset": Rolls back damage taken by the protagonist within the last 5s once (very long CD). |
| Balance | Cannot eat the Scapegoat Specter's main body (or only eats the transferred portion) to avoid mindless countering. |

**Positioning**: Scaling core piece; encourages "clearing mobs to feed the pet" and "protecting the pet from interruptions."

---

### 2.5 Head-Swapping Specter / Fake Mannequin (Friendly Starter Weapon · Mid-tier)

**Original Skeleton**: Headless mannequins in a mall assembling bodies; swaps heads from behind; the victim doesn't realize they are a ghost; cannot swap heads if faced directly.

**Your Design (Keep)**  
Killing an enemy specter has a chance to "Swap Heads," temporarily gaining that specter's ability.

**Refinement Suggestions**

| Point | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| Trigger | % chance on kill. |
| Duration | 8–12s; can only hold 1 head at a time (new head overrides old head). |
| Visuals | The Head-Swapping Specter changes to a "Headless Mannequin + Enemy's Head". |
| Flavor Rule | Extra high success rate for swapping heads on "Enemies facing away from the player" (rewarding positioning). |
| Synergies | Stealing the Knocker's "Knock" allows for a counter-wave; stealing the Scapegoat's transfer acts as a tanking phase. |

**Positioning**: The most highly entertaining, variable-playstyle piece; strong Bestiary collection appeal.

## III. List of "Specters" to Mine from Original Lore (Ranked by Gameplay Usability)

### 3.1 Prioritize as Level Monsters / Bosses

#### Thralls (C)
- **Ability**: Humanoid mobs, mindless rushing.  
- **Gameplay**: Summons of the Knocker/Domain Bosses; drop "Ghost Money" on death (in-run upgrade material used to raise caps/upgrade friendly specters).  
- **Integration**: Gives A-Class Bosses "adds," ensuring the Scapegoat Specter always has transfer targets; simultaneously acts as a steady economy source.

#### Phantom Infant (C→B, Scaling)
- **Ability**: Contact trigger; biting applies a "Targeted" mark; grows into a youth/opens a mini-domain upon maturing.  
- **Gameplay**: Disappears for a while after targeting you, reappearing at a higher growth stage, forcing players to kill it quickly without stalling.  
- **Integration**: The "food chain" narrative for the Ravenous Specter—the friendly Ravenous Specter hard-counters the Phantom Infant.

#### Head-Swapping Mannequin Swarm (B)
- **Ability**: Stationary head-turning; only attacks the player's back; freezes when looked at.  
- **Gameplay**: Forced camera/positioning game; turning your back to a dense cluster = suicide.  
- **Integration**: Mall theme mobs; forms a mirror gag with the friendly Head-Swapping Specter.

#### Footsteps Behind You (B)
- **Ability**: Generates invisible pressure behind the player while moving; approaches and attacks if movement stops.  
- **Gameplay**: The longer you stop/walk, the higher the damage taken; strongly tied to the "Mobile Tower" core mechanics.  
- **Integration**: Fog level killer; can use the Phantom Noose to hoist the "sound source" to reveal it.

#### Reflection Specter (A)
- **Ability**: Reflections in mirrors/puddles attack the physical body; true form only revealed when all reflective surfaces are smashed.  
- **Gameplay**: Smash environmental reflective objects (interactable boxes/mirrors) first, then DPS.  
- **Integration**: Hotel/Bathroom theme level; vision items can "illuminate reflections."

#### Portrait Specter / Huanggang "Mimicry" Echo (A~S)
- **Ability**: Suppresses other specters' abilities; or "mimics" the entire map as a normal village before suddenly turning hostile.  
- **Gameplay**: Periodically silences your 3 friendly specters for 3s; or disguises as chests/NPCs.  
- **Integration**: Deserted Village Level Boss; counters "mindless gear stacking."

#### Nightmare (A/S Event)
- **Ability**: Die if you fall asleep; incorporeal.  
- **Gameplay**: Level rule "No standing still for too long"—standing idle for >N seconds fills a sleep gauge; massive damage when full.  
- **Integration**: Aligns completely with the mobile tower philosophy; enforces "always be moving."

#### Phantom Caller (B Rule-Based Mob, Use with Caution)
- **Ability**: Ringing means death (causality-law vibe).  
- **Gameplay**: Don't make it an instakill; change to "Random phones ring on the map, AoE damage to everyone while ringing, stops when you find and smash the phone booth."  
- **Integration**: Urban street level event wave.

#### Wishing Specter (A, Double-Edged)
- **Ability**: Grants wishes but extracts a toll.  
- **Gameplay**: Optional event: Instantly heal to full/buff attack, but the next wave of enemies is +50% stronger, or the Awakening Gauge spikes.  
- **Integration**: A fun toggle for gambler-type players; avoid making it the single optimal strategy.

#### Ghost Constable (A/S)
- **Ability**: Law enforcement/soul-reaping vibe, specifically targets "Specter Hosts."  
- **Gameplay**: Prioritizes chasing the protagonist, ignoring mobs; deals bonus damage to friendly specters.  
- **Integration**: Multi-level long-term "Wanted Level" Boss.

#### Specter in the Painting (S Vibe)
- **Ability**: Drags people into the painted world.  
- **Gameplay**: Stepping on a canvas area sucks you into a "Painted Instance" mini-room; must shoot your way out within a time limit.  
- **Integration**: Art Gallery/Old Mansion level; adds pacing variety.

#### Supernatural Bus (Environment Boss)
- **Ability**: The bus cabin is the domain; infinite loops if doors don't open.  
- **Gameplay**: The level takes place inside a moving cabin; arriving at a stop + opening doors = temporary safe zone; missing a stop = a harder cabin section.  
- **Integration**: Vertical or segmented maps, naturally suited for "Collect 3 items to get off the bus."

## VI. Protagonist Personality (A playable persona, not a novel copy-paste)

### 6.1 Suggested Personality Keywords

**Calm, foul-mouthed, pragmatic, holds grudges but doesn't act out immediately, prioritizes survival above all, treats rule-analysis as a puzzle.**

Vibes to borrow from the original novel's protagonist (Yang Jian):  
- Forces himself to analyze patterns even when terrified;  
- Doesn't play the hero, dares to collect "hush money" but will still save people;  
- Can tolerate humiliation, but shows absolutely no mercy to specters or those who scheme against him;  
- Humor is his pressure valve (The "cute" Ravenous Specter route fits this perfectly).

### 6.2 How to "Act It Out" In-Game (Low Cost)

- **Short Barks/Voiceovers**: Entering a door: "Another one of these places." Knocking warning: "Don't answer that." Successful extraction: "Made a profit."  
- **Behavioral Feedback**: Humiliating failure text that isn't melodramatic ("You died. Stop spacing out next time.").  
- **Choice Flavor**: Wishing event options always carry a cost, reflecting his "There's no such thing as free goodwill" philosophy.

The protagonist doesn't need to be named Yang Jian; use organizational titles like "Archive Codename: Phantom Eye" to maintain a healthy distance from the source material.
