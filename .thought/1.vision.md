# Chest Rush · One-Page Pitch

> Based on the `.thought/godot/0.recipe.md` template. See `0.story.md` for the world-building, and `2.next_phase.md` for phase goals. This document is the baseline for all subsequent trade-offs.

## The Six Positioning Questions

**Who is the player, and who do they play as?**
A **Specter Host** in a supernatural-awakening city: using their own body as a cage to harness evil spirits, diving through "Doors" into specter domain fragments. Lightly equipped, fast-paced, relying on positioning and build scaling to win.

**What does the player primarily do in each run?**
Starts by bringing 3 friendly specters (passive auto-battlers), smashing chests in a fixed-layout specter domain to loot gold and key items; uses gold for in-run upgrades to raise stat caps; under the pressure of constantly spawning and steadily strengthening enemy waves, gathers all key items and dashes to the "Door" to extract.

**What is the most compelling conflict or trade-off?**
**Firepower Scaling Curve vs. Monster Strengthening Curve**—when to upgrade, what to pick up, and whether to risk going for further chests. The harder you loot, the stronger your tower gets, but the longer you stay, the higher the enemy pressure. The strategy lies in the pacing of your build, not in the binary choice of "to extract or not."

**What is the expected length of a single run?**
5–10 minutes, suited for fragmented/bite-sized play sessions.

**What emotions do we want the player to repeatedly experience?**
The sense of control over the mobile tower's scaling, the tension of positioning under wave pressure, the release of gathering everything and extracting through the door, and the "just one more run" feeling of "I can optimize my scaling curve better next time."

**What is explicitly NOT being done in this project (Current Phase)?**
Multi-level maps, level selection UI, narrative art, visual map editors, multiplayer networking. Until the single-level game feel and stat curves are stable, we will not start major work on long-term progression systems.

## One-Sentence Pitch

A tower defense game wearing the skin of an extraction shooter: Specter Hosts use 3 specters as passive firepower and themselves as a mobile tower, racing against the scaling curve in a specter domain, and extracting through a door after gathering key relics.

## Gray Box Prototype Key Decisions (Still Valid)

- **Presentation**: Solid color blocks + Chinese HUD, no art assets; validating "fun" rather than "pretty".
- **Controls**: WASD/Arrow keys to move; 3 Specters auto-target and fire; Q to use vision items; 1/2/3 to buy upgrades (raises cap and fully restores); R to restart after the tally screen. Drops are auto-looted on contact.
- **Map**: Defined by 40×24 ASCII grid; walls `#` permanently block vision, obstacles `o` are destructible (block vision while intact), chests `C` are destroyed by multi-hit attacks; extraction point = "Door".
- **Fog of War**: Tri-state (Unexplored/Explored/Visible), initial vision radius of 3 tiles, BFS spread, blocked by walls; monsters and drops are only rendered on visible tiles.
- **Combat & Waves**: Passive attacks; waves spawn randomly in a ring around the player, quantities start slow then ramp up steeply.
- **Upgrades**: 3 paths (ATK / SPD / HP), costs scale incrementally; HP upgrade = raises max HP and heals to full instantly.
- **Clear Condition**: Collect 3 Key Items → Unlock Extraction → Step on the Door to leave. Key items extracted can be used for cross-run permanent progression (the pillar of the long-term loop, see `2.next_phase.md`).
