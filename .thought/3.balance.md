- ATK Lv 0: Blade 36 > Bolt 20 > Aura 5

## II. Balance Model (Target Ranges)

Tower Defense = A race between two curves. **Friendly DPS growth must always slightly trail behind monster HP growth**. Players must continuously upgrade to maintain parity, and are pushed toward extraction in the late game.

- Time to Kill (TTK) = Monster HP / Friendly DPS. Wave 1 ≈ 70/61 ≈ 1.1s; Wave 10 without upgrades ≈ 627/61 ≈ 10s (Upgrades are mandatory).
- Death Point Target: Standing still with no inputs should result in death by **Wave 6–8**; real human positioning + upgrades should sustain until Wave 10–12 and allow extraction.
- Economy: Upgrades must require trade-offs; players shouldn't easily max out levels just by sweeping chests.
