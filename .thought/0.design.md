# Search, fight, retreat: core gameplay and minimalist design points of the game

## I. Minimalist Description of Core Gameplay (Purely Based on the Basic Rules of Matches)

The game features a fixed-level search and combat gameplay, adopting a multi-themed fixed-level design inspired by "Defense of the Radish 4". Each level boasts its own unique map layout, scenic landform, and monster characteristics. It is equipped with a fixed, exclusive starting equipment system and item refresh rules, ensuring that the terrain and monster spawn points are all predictable and fixed. All obstacles and treasure chests on the map can be destroyed, steadily dropping coins and various items. Monsters on the map are periodically refreshed at **fixed intervals** at designated points, distinguishing themselves from the clearing and settlement rounds. The monsters on the field do not need to be cleared and can be retained and accumulated continuously, with their overall stats steadily increasing with each timed round, compelling players to enhance their combat power in a timely manner while continuously gathering resources. To prevent players from deliberately retaining task items and engaging in an endless cycle of infinitely refreshing rounds without evacuating, the necessary task items and common items for the game are hidden in treasure chests. Different levels feature exclusive monster characteristics that precisely counter and adapt to the fixed starting equipment of the game, forcing players to develop exclusive strategies for combat, gathering, and evacuation based on the characteristics of each level. In-game coins are only used to **temporarily enhance** the starting equipment and item stats within the game, with the enhancement cost increasing progressively and having a level cap. All enhancement effects are reset after the game ends. Players start with only 3-grid vision, with scene vision obscured (refer to the fog mechanism of "Defense of the Radish 4"). Vision can be expanded by consuming vision-expanding items, one grid at a time. Wall obstacles permanently block vision, and destroying obstacles and treasure chests requires multiple attacks. The map features multiple differentiated evacuation paths, each with different monster densities, obstacle counts, and resource distributions. Players must complete designated gathering tasks to unlock evacuation point permissions; after completing the gathering tasks, the stats of monsters on the field (attack speed and movement speed) will skyrocket, and a fixed-duration high-pressure evacuation countdown will start. If the player fails to evacuate within the time limit, the game will be directly lost, ultimately forming a complete and flawless exclusive game loop of "covert gathering and development - timed anti-monster combat and power enhancement - limited-time high-pressure evacuation".

## II. Supplementary Points for Companion Addictive Design

Low-pressure, rhythmic, and repetitive background music (BGM) creates an immersive atmosphere. A clear and unified core objective eliminates internal decision-making friction. The entire process, from destruction/killing/collection/upgrading, provides instant positive reward feedback. Gameplay and rewards are highly predictable and unified. The game features diverse equipment, monsters, and levels, offering varied gameplay experiences. Visible combat power growth enhances the sense of control. Gradually increasing difficulty caters to both novice and advanced players. A guaranteed reward mechanism reduces the frustration of defeat. A long-term retention system for collecting achievements simplifies gameplay for fragmented playtime. Clearing levels unlocks a global catalog and new starting equipment.
