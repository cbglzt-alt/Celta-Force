# Chest Rush · Next Phase Design Correction (Post-Playtest, 2026-07-28)

> Source: Gray box prototype actual playtest feedback. This document corrects outdated assumptions in `1.vision.md` and defines the next phase.
> Old text is kept for historical record; in case of conflict, this document prevails.

## I. Repositioning the Essence of the Game

**This game is a tower defense wearing the skin of an extraction shooter: the protagonist is a mobile tower.**

- There is no "keep looting / extract now" greed vs. extraction gamble—that was a previous assumption, falsified by playtesting.
- The true core loop: **Passive attack → Stat check → Gather 3 key items before monsters get too strong → Extract through the "Door"**.
- The strategy is not about "to extract or not", but "**Firepower Scaling Curve vs. Monster Strengthening Curve**": the pacing of your build, timing of upgrades, what to pick up, and whether you dare to go for further chests.

## II. Background Setting (For tone-setting, no narrative content)

- An alternate world overrun by supernatural anomalies (inspired by the vibe of "Mysterious Awakening").
- The protagonist is a "Specter Host", who passes through "Doors" into specter domains to collect key items, enhancing their power to save the world.
- **Starts each level with 3 "Specters"** = 3 passive attack equipments, each specter represents an attack ability.
- Monsters in the specter domain are "specters" with various abilities that actively attack the protagonist.
- Key items = **Long-term progression resources across levels** (can permanently upgrade oneself after successfully extracting them).

## III. Hard Changes Confirmed by Playtesting (Must-do for the Next Phase)

1. **Passive Attack**: Removed the manual attack button. Weapons/Specters auto-target and auto-fire. Players only manage positioning and looting. This is the foundation that makes the "mobile tower" work.
2. **Monsters spawn randomly on the spot**: Waves spawn in random positions around the player, with numbers significantly increasing each wave, escalating the pressure.
3. **Upgrades changed to raising the cap**: In-run gold upgrades = raise max cap and instantly heal to full (e.g., buying HP → Max HP +N and heal to full once). The perception must be strong; say goodbye to squeezing toothpaste.
4. **Fix late-game stat collapse**: Current upgrade returns lag far behind late-game monster strengthening, making the late game too difficult. Rework the scaling curve to ensure upgrades can keep up with wave intensity.
5. **3-Specter Equipment System**: A fixed starting loadout of 3 attack equipments (3 specters) per level. Loadouts are fixed by level, reflecting counter/adaptation synergies.

## IV. Cross-Level Long-Term Progression (New Pillar, Connecting Multiple Levels)

- Key items extracted can **permanently upgrade personal stats across levels**.
- This upgrades the "single-level closed loop" to a "multi-level long term": collecting is no longer just a clear condition, but an investment in growth.
- It aligns seamlessly with the "global bestiary unlock and new starting equipment upon clearing" and "collection achievements for long-term retention" in `0.design.md`.
